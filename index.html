Ryan Lee

CS PhD Student, USC Information Sciences Institute

I'm a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Southern California, working at the Information Sciences Institute with Jonathan May.

I study compute-adaptive language models: architectures that allocate computation per token rather than applying a fixed depth to every input. My recent work shows that sparse mixture-of-experts layers are critical to scaling looped language models.

Before grad school, I worked in hardware design at Microsoft (Surface Slim Pen 2) and Apple (Touch ID on the M2 MacBook Air). I hold a bachelor's and master's in Mechanical Engineering from Stanford.
